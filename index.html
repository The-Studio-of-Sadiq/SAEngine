Structural analysis and design, in one open program.

SAEngine takes a building from the soil report to the analysis, the member design, the drawings, the bill of quantities and the calculation report. It is free software for practising structural engineers, and your projects stay on your computer.

What it does

  • Model

Model

One New Project wizard sets up the grid and storeys. Draw columns, beams, walls and slabs on the floor plan, with typical floors and a 3D view.

Analysis

Finite element analysis with OpenSees: static, modal and response spectrum, with the load combinations of the chosen code.

Design

Reinforced concrete beams, columns, slabs, walls, footings, rafts, piles and pile caps, with every value traced to its clause.

Codes

Code packs, not hard-coded rules: BNBC 2020 by default, with ACI/ASCE, Eurocode, IS and more as packs.

Outputs

DXF drawings (DWG through LibreDWG, editing in LibreCAD), the BOQ with cement bags and sand and stone volumes, and a calculation report.

Working together

Share a project through the office's own file server, with versions, part locks and live co-editing over a relay on the office network.

Your data stays yours

Local first

Projects are folders on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to.

Never sold

Diagnostics to the developers are off by default, and you see everything before it is sent.

Your office, your relay

Sharing and co-editing use your office's own server or network, never a third-party cloud.

Report a problem or help out

In SAEngine, use Help › Report a Bug or Send Feedback. You can also open an issue (see Reporting problems), which is public. For anything about a client, a site or a project you cannot share, e-mail publicrelations@thestudioofsadiq.tech.

To help build SAEngine, start with Contributing. Work in progress and the plan are on the repository: main holds releases and beta the work towards the next one.
