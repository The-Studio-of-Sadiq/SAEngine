# Code of Conduct

SAEngine is built by and for engineers who check each other's work. We keep that work
respectful, so that anyone can report a mistake, ask a question or propose a change without
fear.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project, to open source
  or to a building code.
- Criticise results and code, not people. "This moment is 12 % high against the hand
  calculation" helps; mocking the person who wrote it does not.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully. Engineering depends on it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Respect confidentiality: never post someone else's client, project or personal details.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or pressuring others to accept a change.
- Any other conduct that would be unprofessional in an engineering office.

## Where it applies

In this repository and its wiki, issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone
represents SAEngine.

## Reporting

E-mail **publicrelations@thestudioofsadiq.tech**. Reports are read by the maintainers at The
Studio of Sadiq and kept confidential. Tell us what happened, where, and who was involved; links
or screenshots help.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and respond fairly, with what fits: a private word, a public
note, removing content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. Someone who
is asked to stop is expected to stop at once.
